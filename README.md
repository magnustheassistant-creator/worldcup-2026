# 2026 FIFA World Cup — Full Bracket

All 12 groups, all 48 teams, predictions for who advances.

- **Live page:** https://magnustheassistant-creator.github.io/worldcup-2026/
- **Source:** HTML generated from FIFA rankings (Nov 2025) and Wikipedia tournament pages
- **Compiled:** June 10, 2026

## What's here

- Every group A–L, with all 4 teams per group
- FIFA ranking, confederation, pot, finals appearances, best historical finish
- Predicted qualifiers (top 2 per group) — heuristic, not bookmaker odds
- Co-hosts (Mexico, Canada, United States) and 4 debut teams (Curaçao, Cape Verde, Jordan, Uzbekistan) flagged
- Tournament basics: format, dates, host cities

## Data sources

- FIFA World Rankings (Nov 2025) — used for the draw seeding
- Wikipedia: [2026 FIFA World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_FIFA_World_Cup) and the per-group pages
- Country flags: [flagcdn.com](https://flagcdn.com)
